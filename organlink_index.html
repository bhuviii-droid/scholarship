How it works

Awareness first. Safe action always.

Learn the basic journey and understand why consent, medical review and authorised transplant systems matter.

📖

1. Learn

Understand organ donation, the importance of informed consent and the role of authorised transplant organisations.

📝

2. Express interest

Use the sample form to explore a donor-interest registration workflow. This demo stores sample entries only in this browser.

🏥

3. Contact authorised services

Patients and families should contact a registered transplant hospital or the appropriate government-authorised network.

Important medical and legal note

Organ suitability, consent, compatibility assessment and allocation must be handled by qualified medical professionals and authorised transplant systems under applicable law. Never use a public directory to offer, request, buy, sell or allocate organs. For urgent medical needs, contact a treating doctor or transplant hospital.
